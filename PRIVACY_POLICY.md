# Atelier Time — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 30, 2026  
**App developer:** Avi Yosfan

Atelier Time is an offline class-management timer for live-model drawing and sculpting workshops. It does not require an account, and it does not automatically upload saved classes, sound choices or diagnostic Run reports to the developer.

## 1. What information the app processes

The app processes information you create or select to operate the timer, including class names, class schedules, pose and break durations, end-time choices, saved classes, and sound preferences.

When you run a class, the app can generate diagnostic **Run reports** in private storage on your device. Reports contain class names and rules, timing events and user actions, sound-selection metadata, and basic device context: Android API version, device manufacturer and model, app version, and timezone. These reports **do not contain** the audio content you select or the selected sound file's document URI.

## 2. Local storage; automatic collection and transfer

Saved classes, sound settings, user-selected sound-file references and Run reports are stored in Android application-private storage on the user's device. The reviewed app runs without an account or internet connection, does not contain developer-operated cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics uploads, and disables Android app backup.

The app does **not automatically transmit** the above locally stored information to the developer or to a developer-operated server.

## 3. User-controlled export and sharing

If you choose **Export** for a Run report, Android asks you to select a file destination. You can choose local device storage or a document provider, which may be operated by another app or cloud service. The exported file leaves Atelier Time's private storage and may be subject to that provider's privacy practices. You decide whether to export a report and where to put it; Atelier Time does not automatically upload reports.

## 4. Permissions, notifications and selected sounds

Atelier Time may request Android notification permission to show a live class-status notification. Declining this permission does not prevent ordinary class timing and cue playback; it may prevent the status notification from being displayed.

During an active class, the app may use an Android foreground service to display up-to-date status, support the application process while it is in the background, and remove the status notification on task dismissal. This is not a guarantee that the Android system will never interrupt the app.

If you select your own sound file, the app uses Android's system document picker for the specific file you choose. It does not request general browsing access to your media library. The optional keep-screen-awake setting operates while the app is visible.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Local information remains available across ordinary app updates. You can delete saved classes and individual Run diagnostic reports through their respective in-app controls. When you uninstall the app, Android normally removes the app's private data; **previously exported files** are not automatically deleted and must be removed separately at their chosen destination.

## 6. Security

Atelier Time uses Android application-private storage and Android's document picker for user-chosen external files. No account, password or developer-operated online service is required. No device or software environment can be guaranteed risk-free.

## 7. Other users and children

Atelier Time is intended for organizing workshop class timing. It does not ask for account registration, a user's birth date or other sign-up information. Workshop organizers should avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive details about participants in class names or exported reports.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
