Atelier Time

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Atelier Time handles information when you use its drawing and sculpting class timers.

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Who operates this app?

Atelier Time is developed by Avi Yosfan. For privacy questions or requests, contact Avi_yosfan@walla.com.

2. Accounts and internet use

Atelier Time is designed to operate offline. You do not need to register, sign in or create an account. The reviewed Android app does not automatically transmit your saved classes, sound selections or run reports to the developer, and does not contain developer-operated cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics upload.

3. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, the app stores saved class names and schedules, selected sound preferences and references to user-chosen audio files, design settings, and run histories in Android app-private storage on your device.

Run diagnostic reports can contain class names and schedules, timestamps and timing events, sound selection metadata, and basic device details used for troubleshooting: Android API version, manufacturer, device model, app version and time zone. Diagnostic reports do not include the full contents of your selected audio files, and intentionally omit their file URI identifiers.

4. Permissions, notifications and audio

On supported Android versions, the app may request notification permission to display an ongoing class-status notification. If you decline, you can still use the class timer and audible cues. During an active class, a foreground service can support timely status and background continuity; it does not upload class information to a server.

You can choose sounds provided by the app, system sounds or individual audio files using Android's system picker. If you select a personal file, Android grants the app access to that selected item, not your entire media library. The optional keep-screen-awake setting affects only device display behavior.

5. Sharing and exporting reports

The app does not automatically send your data to the developer or any third party. You can choose to export a run diagnostic report using Android's document-save interface. Only after you initiate Export and select a destination is a copy written to that destination. Depending on your choice, the destination may be another app, a device folder or a cloud-backed document provider. Any handling outside Atelier Time is governed by that destination's privacy practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Saved information remains in the app's local storage while you use it, including across normal in-place app updates. You can delete individual saved classes and run reports using the app's controls. Android's system app-data deletion and uninstall features may remove app-private data. Files you explicitly exported are stored outside the app's private area and must be deleted at their chosen destinations separately.

7. Security

Data is stored using Android's private app-storage facilities. The app's reviewed Android configuration disables app backup. As with any local app, security also depends on your device settings and operating system. The app does not operate a remote user-data database.

8. External links

The About screen may include a link to the developer's Instagram profile. Opening this link leaves Atelier Time; Instagram's own policies apply to information you share with or view on that service. The app does not embed the Instagram feed or require an Instagram sign-in.

9. Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when app features or data handling change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected in this policy and, where required, in the app or store listing.

10. Contact

Privacy contact: Avi_yosfan@walla.com.
